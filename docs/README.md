# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is an index to OctoAcme’s project management guidance.

## Process overview

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, structured approach that guides work from idea through delivery and continuous improvement. Initiation validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success measures, and captures an initial timeline and risks. After approval, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and release plans.

Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication; Product Managers set goals, prioritize work, and focus teams on customer and business value. Developers build and test features, maintain documentation, participate in planning and reviews, and surface technical risks. QA and stakeholders help validate quality, confirm acceptance criteria, and provide feedback or approvals.

During execution, teams use a visible project board, regular standups and delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos. Risks and blockers are tracked and escalated as needed, while weekly or milestone-based updates keep stakeholders informed. Quality is built in through automated and manual testing, CI checks, and reviewed pull requests. Releases require validated acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, rollback planning, and staged verification. Retrospectives after sprints, milestones, or incidents turn lessons into trackable improvements.
